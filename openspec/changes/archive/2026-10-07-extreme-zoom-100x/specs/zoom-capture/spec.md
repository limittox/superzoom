# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Maximum zoom bound by native pixels
The app SHALL limit the maximum zoom to the lower of 100x and the zoom at which the captured crop's short side would fall below 64 native sensor pixels. The maximum therefore depends on the device's sensor resolution and longest lens.

#### Scenario: Maximum reached
- **WHEN** the user zooms to the app's maximum on any supported device
- **THEN** a capture at that zoom produces a crop whose short side is at least 64 pixels

#### Scenario: Phone with a 5x telephoto
- **WHEN** the longest lens is 5x and produces 12.5 MP photos
- **THEN** the maximum zoom is 100x

#### Scenario: Phone limited to its main lens
- **WHEN** hardware zoom is capped at 1x and the main lens produces 12 MP photos
- **THEN** the maximum zoom is below 100x, and a capture at the maximum still has a short side of at least 64 pixels

### Requirement: Zoom level indicator
The app SHALL always show the current zoom factor, and SHALL show in a visually distinct way which of three ranges it is in: optical (within the longest lens), AI zoom (beyond optical, up to the native-pixel limit), or AI-reconstructed (beyond the native-pixel limit, where most detail is generated).

#### Scenario: Entering the beyond-optical range
- **WHEN** the zoom passes the longest lens's native factor
- **THEN** the indicator changes to show that the zoom is beyond the optical range

#### Scenario: Entering the AI-reconstructed range
- **WHEN** the zoom passes the native-pixel limit
- **THEN** the indicator changes again and a short warning says most detail will be generated by AI

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Native-pixel limit
The app SHALL compute a native-pixel limit: the zoom at which the captured crop holds 1 megapixel of native sensor pixels. Captures beyond it SHALL be marked as AI-reconstructed.

#### Scenario: Capture beyond the limit
- **WHEN** the native-pixel limit is 14x and the user captures at 40x
- **THEN** the capture is marked as AI-reconstructed

#### Scenario: Capture within the limit
- **WHEN** the native-pixel limit is 14x and the user captures at 10x
- **THEN** the capture is not marked as AI-reconstructed

### Requirement: Extreme-zoom preview
The app SHALL request preview stabilization from the camera where the device supports it. Past the native-pixel limit, it SHALL show a hint to brace the phone or rest it on something.

#### Scenario: Zooming far past the limit
- **WHEN** the zoom is beyond the native-pixel limit
- **THEN** a brace-the-phone hint is shown above the controls

#### Scenario: Stabilization unsupported
- **WHEN** the camera does not support preview stabilization
- **THEN** the preview still works without it
